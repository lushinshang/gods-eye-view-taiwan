# Official/Institutional Sources for JCG and Chinese Research Vessel Rosters (vs AIS Aggregators)

## Does Japan Coast Guard (kaiho.mlit.go.jp) publish a complete public roster of its patrol vessel fleet (ship names, hull numbers, classes)?

### Takeaway
JCG's official site (kaiho.mlit.go.jp) publishes vessel **class/type examples** and annual **fleet-size totals** in its public pamphlet, and a dedicated "船艇" page listing representative vessel names per class, but neither source gives a complete name-by-name roster with hull numbers, and neither includes MMSI or call signs.

### Cited Findings
- JCG's 2026 pamphlet (dated 2026-04-01) states the total fleet is 475 vessels/craft: 149 巡視船 (patrol vessels), 67 特殊警備救難艇 (special security/rescue craft), 237 巡視艇 (patrol craft), plus other categories — but this is an aggregate count, not a name list. — [2026 JCG Pamphlet](https://www.kaiho.mlit.go.jp/doc/hakkou/pamphlet/2026_ja_01.pdf)
- JCG's "船艇" (vessels/craft) page lists representative/example vessel names per hull class (e.g., PLH-class 「ゆみはり」, PL-class 3,500-ton 「おおすみ」, PM-class 500-ton 「ちとせ」, PS-class 180-ton 「たかちほ」, PC/CL patrol craft like 「よど」「きたぐも」「ことなみ」「やまぎく」「はやかぜ」), but it is illustrative by class rather than an exhaustive name-by-hull-number list. — [JCG 船艇ページ](https://www.kaiho.mlit.go.jp/soubi-yosan/sentei/ship.html)
- Neither page lists hull numbers (e.g., "PL-XX"), MMSI, or call signs for any vessel. — [JCG 船艇ページ](https://www.kaiho.mlit.go.jp/soubi-yosan/sentei/ship.html)
- Individual vessel commissioning/retirement announcements are published as separate news items (e.g., はてるま PL-94 delivered 2024-02-22 to 石垣海上保安部; its predecessor はてるま PL-61 renamed だいせつ and transferred to 紋別海上保安部 on 2023-12-15; やえやま PL-203 launched Nov 2022) — these do include hull numbers but are scattered across individual press releases, not a consolidated roster. — [trafficnews.jp 記事](https://trafficnews.jp/post/131175), [trafficnews.jp 記事](https://trafficnews.jp/post/123091)

### Inferences
- A complete hull-number roster likely exists only internally or scattered across individual commissioning press releases (dated news items per vessel); reconstructing one would require aggregating years of JCG press releases rather than a single page.
- Since JCG's own site contains zero MMSI/call-sign data, the Japanese government's MMSI-to-name linkage for JCG vessels can only be resolved via the 総務省 radio-license search (see next section), not via kaiho.mlit.go.jp itself.

### Gaps
- No single JCG page enumerating all ~475 vessels/craft by individual name + hull number was found. This would need either a FOIA-style request, aggregating yearly budget/procurement PDFs (e.g., tender announcements found at kaiho.mlit.go.jp/*/tenders/*.pdf), or searching JCG's annual white paper (海上保安庁 白書) which may have a full vessel appendix — not checked in this pass due to tool-call budget.

## Is there a Japanese radio station license public lookup (総務省 無線局等情報検索) usable to find a named vessel's MMSI/call sign from an official government database?

### Takeaway
Yes — 総務省 operates a public, keyless lookup called **無線局等情報検索** (Radio Station Information Search) under the 電波利用ホームページ/電波利用ポータル, reachable via the portal's 「検索・統計」→「無線局等情報検索」 menu, and there is also a documented Web-API (JSON/XML/CSV) for the same data (https://www.tele.soumu.go.jp/j/musen/webapi/). However, this tool was not successfully queried in this research pass (the exact direct search URL/query parameters were not confirmed via WebFetch within budget), so it should be noted as "found but not yet exercised."

### Cited Findings
- A 総務省 PDF confirms the legal basis: since the FY2002 Radio Act amendment (電波法 Article 25), licence-record details (license date, licensee name, prefecture/municipality of installation, frequency, antenna power) are published online via the 電波利用ホームページ, with personal addresses and precise install locations withheld for privacy. — [総務省 PDF](https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_content/000551755.pdf)
- A 総務省 slide deck describes the navigation path: from the 電波利用ホームページ's 「検索・統計」screen, select 「無線局等情報検索」, then search by frequency/region etc. — [総務省スライド資料](https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_content/000852610.pdf)
- Regional 総合通信局 pages (e.g., 近畿, 東北) link out to 無線局等情報検索 on the 電波利用ポータル for small-vessel ("特定船舶局"/MSS and "無線航行移動局"/RO) license lookups, and note that as of 2025-10-01 paper license certificates were discontinued in favor of digital records viewable on the 電波利用ポータル. — [近畿総合通信局ページ](https://www.soumu.go.jp/soutsu/kinki/dempa/maritime.html)
- A government API catalog entry confirms a public Web-API mirroring this search exists, documented at https://www.tele.soumu.go.jp/j/musen/webapi/, supporting JSON/XML/CSV responses. — [api-catalog.e-gov.go.jp](https://api-catalog.e-gov.go.jp/info/en/apicatalog/view/46)

### Inferences
- This tool is designed for licensee-side lookups (frequency, region, licensee name) rather than an MMSI-by-vessel-name reverse lookup interface — it may not be structured to answer "what is this named ship's MMSI" directly, since published fields emphasize license administrative data over vessel identity cross-referencing. This is an inference, not confirmed, since the live search interface itself was not reached.

### Gaps
- The exact direct URL and required query parameters for 無線局等情報検索 were not confirmed (only the navigation path and the Web-API documentation page were found). A follow-up researcher should navigate https://www.tele.soumu.go.jp/j/proc/diglic/ or https://www.tele.soumu.go.jp/j/musen/webapi/ interactively (likely requires JavaScript-rendered forms, which plain WebFetch could not execute) to actually test a named-vessel search and confirm whether JCG patrol vessels (which are government, not civilian, radio stations) even appear in this public-facing database, or whether government vessels are exempted from public disclosure.

## Do Chinese operating institutions (自然资源部/国家海洋局分局, 中科院海洋研究所, 广州海洋地质调查局, 厦门大学) publish official fleet roster pages with vessel names, specs, and identifying numbers?

### Takeaway
Institutional websites (e.g., 厦门大学 ships.xmu.edu.cn) do publish detailed single-vessel specification pages (dimensions, tonnage, builder, mission history), but none found in this pass publish MMSI or call signs, and no consolidated multi-vessel fleet roster page covering all of 自然资源部's subordinate institutes was found — coverage is fragmented per-institute, per-vessel news/specification pages.

### Cited Findings
- 厦门大学"嘉庚"号 (Tan Kah Kee) has a dedicated official site (ships.xmu.edu.cn) with full specs: 3,000-ton class, unlimited navigation area, laid down 2015, launched/named 2016, delivered to XMU and inducted into the national ocean survey fleet April 2017; overall length 77.7m, beam 16.24m, gross tonnage ~3,517t, endurance ~10,000nm, self-sustainability 50 days, all-electric propulsion; concept/preliminary design by US firm Glosten, detailed design by CSSC's 708th Research Institute, built by Guangzhou Shipyard International (广船国际). As of a Dec 2025 announcement, it has completed 70+ research cruises serving 2,500+ researchers. No MMSI or call sign is published on these pages. — [ships.xmu.edu.cn 介绍页](https://ships.xmu.edu.cn/jghkkc1/jzls1.htm), [mel2.xmu.edu.cn 设备页](https://mel2.xmu.edu.cn/melwebold/facility.asp?id=32)
- 中科院海洋研究所's "科学" (Ke Xue) vessel is covered via institute news (e.g., 科技日报 2026-07-04 report on departure for a West Pacific shared research cruise) describing it as a domestically designed/built new-generation comprehensive research vessel with global navigation capability (10+ equator-crossing cruises as of 2022), but no dedicated official spec page with hull numbers/MMSI was found in this pass. — [科技日报 2026-07-04](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-07/04/content_542108.html)
- 中国地质调查局 (CGS) and its subordinate 广州海洋地质调查局 publish per-vessel mission news (e.g., "海洋地质六号" completing its 15th deep-sea geological survey cruise leg 2, using a self-developed 6,000m-class ROV, planning a 2026 H1 return to the Pacific; "海洋六号" completing its 8th deep-sea geology/55th China Ocean cruise in 2019, 122 days, ~35,000km, operated specifically by 广州海洋地质调查局) but no consolidated roster page, and specifically **no page describing "海洋地质八号" (Haiyang Dizhi No. 8) was found** in this search pass. — [中国新闻网 2025-12-10](https://www.chinanews.com/gn/2025/12-10/10530262.shtml), [中国日报网 2019-10-23](https://cn.chinadaily.com.cn/a/201910/23/WS5dafc985a31099ab995e74b7.html)
- A third-party trade publication (Baird Maritime, not an official institutional source but citing CGS) describes a related vessel "Haiyang Dizhi No 26," operated by CGS's 海口海洋地质调查中心, as 63.5m long, 12.6m beam, carrying 34 crew/scientists, max 35-day endurance — included here only as corroborating context, not as an official-source finding. — [Baird Maritime](https://bairdmaritime.com/?p=304193)
- A 船舶工程 (Ship Engineering) academic journal 2024 article reportedly compiles data on China's major ocean research vessels by category, which could serve as a secondary cross-reference, but its full text/data table was not retrieved in this pass. — [船舶工程期刊摘要页](https://search.napstic.cn/literature/periodical/010cbgc202409004)

### Inferences
- Chinese institutional websites prioritize mission/achievement narratives over static identifying-data rosters; MMSI/call sign data for these vessels is essentially absent from official institutional sources in what was retrievable here, meaning cross-referencing against AIS aggregator-reported MMSI values cannot be verified from the Chinese institutional side with the sources found.
- "海洋地质八号" specifically returned no official-source hits in this pass — it either operates under a different/renamed designation, is less publicly documented than sister vessels 六号/二十六号, or requires a more targeted follow-up search.

### Gaps
- No official 自然资源部, 国家海洋局北海分局/东海分局/南海分局, or CGS/广州海洋地质调查局 consolidated multi-vessel fleet roster page was located (only piecemeal single-vessel mission announcements).
- "海洋地质8号" (Haiyang Dizhi 8) was not found in official sources in this pass — a dedicated follow-up search using CGS's own site search or 广州海洋地质调查局's own domain is recommended.
- No MMSI, call sign, or IMO number appears on any official institutional page found; all MMSI values for these vessels remain attributable only to third-party AIS aggregators (MagicPort, VesselFinder, Maritime Optima, etc.), not to institutional primary sources.

## Has any academic maritime security research paper published a methodology appendix with a verified Chinese research vessel list (vs. news articles)?

### Takeaway
The closest match is a CSIS Futures Lab analysis ("China's Weaponization of Dual-Use Research Vessels for Undersea Data Dominance") which documents a reproducible methodology — starting from the University of Delaware's International Research Vessel Schedules & Information dataset, cross-referencing against MarineTraffic.com for vessel type and MMSI, yielding 223 vessels across 48 countries/5 categories, then querying AIS via General Atomics' Optix platform (2020-01-01 to 2025-12-09) — but the vessel-by-MMSI data table itself is embedded as **images** in Appendices A/B, not machine-readable text, so the actual MMSI values could not be extracted from this pass.

### Cited Findings
- CSIS's methodology: selected fisheries research, oceanographic survey, dive, special, and fishery-patrol vessels; cross-referenced against MarineTraffic.com to verify vessel type and MMSI; resulting dataset = 223 vessels across 48 countries; AIS data queried from General Atomics' Optix platform, Jan 1 2020 – Dec 9 2025. — [CSIS report](https://www.csis.org/analysis/chinas-weaponization-dual-use-research-vessels-undersea-data-dominance)
- The report's two detailed case-study vessels are named in text: **向阳红06 (Xiang Yang Hong 06)** (tracked near Guam) and **科学号 (Ke Xue)** (tracked in the Western Pacific and Bering Sea), both flagged as PRC research vessels exhibiting AIS tracks resembling undersea-cable survey patterns. — [CSIS report](https://www.csis.org/analysis/chinas-weaponization-dual-use-research-vessels-undersea-data-dominance)
- No MMSI or IMO numbers for any vessel (including Xiang Yang Hong 06 or Ke Xue) appear in the report's retrievable text; the underlying vessel-list tables (A1–A4, B1) are rendered as images and were not machine-readable via this pass's fetch. — [CSIS report](https://www.csis.org/analysis/chinas-weaponization-dual-use-research-vessels-undersea-data-dominance)
- The report explicitly caveats that its 223-vessel sample is "not a census of all research ships" — i.e., it is sample-selected, not an exhaustive official roster. — [CSIS report](https://www.csis.org/analysis/chinas-weaponization-dual-use-research-vessels-undersea-data-dominance)
- A separate 2025 academic paper (Wang & Wang, *Natural Science of Hainan University*, DOI 10.15886/j.cnki.hndk.2025041501) addresses a related but distinct problem: detecting vessels broadcasting multiple simultaneous MMSI codes, using an ensemble-learning model validated on Hainan AIS data (reported accuracy 0.96) — relevant methodologically to MMSI-conflict resolution in general, but not specific to Chinese research vessels or to a published vessel-name roster. — [sciopen.com article page](https://www.sciopen.com/article/10.15886/j.cnki.hndk.2025041501)

### Inferences
- Even this academic-adjacent source (CSIS) ultimately relies on MarineTraffic (a commercial AIS aggregator) as its MMSI source of truth — meaning no fully independent, non-aggregator-derived academic MMSI verification method for Chinese research vessels was found in this pass.
- The CSIS PDF (as opposed to the HTML page fetched) may contain the image-based appendix tables in a form that could be OCR'd or manually transcribed by a follow-up researcher with direct PDF access — worth a dedicated follow-up if those 223 vessel/MMSI pairs are needed.

### Gaps
- Could not extract the actual vessel-name-to-MMSI table from CSIS Appendices A/B (image-based, not machine-readable via the fetch tool used).
- No search was done (due to tool budget) for the CSIS report's underlying raw dataset/appendix PDF hosted separately, which might be machine-readable; a follow-up should try fetching the direct PDF URL if available.

## Cross-check: for conflicting MMSI values (雪龙号 412863000 vs 412018330; 实验3号 412988000 vs 413621760; 大洋一号 412923000 vs 412017140), can a third independent source resolve which is correct?

### Takeaway
No definitive resolution was found from official/institutional sources for any of the three conflicts. For 雪龙号 and 大洋一号, multiple AIS aggregators (MarineTraffic-family sites, MagicPort, VesselFinder, Maritime Optima, VesselTracker) disagree with each other, and the most *authoritative* possible tie-breaker identified — **ITU's MARS (Maritime mobile Access and Retrieval System)**, the UN body that actually administers MMSI allocation — could not be successfully queried within this research pass due to the search form requiring interactive/JS-driven submission rather than a simple URL query string. For 实验3号, no source mentioning MMSI 412988000 was found at all in this pass (only 413621760 appeared), so that conflict could not even be characterized, let alone resolved.

### Cited Findings
- **ITU MARS** is described (by ITU's own seminar materials and the US Coast Guard NAVCEN) as the official, free, daily-updated global registry of ship-station notifications (searchable by ship name, call sign, MMSI, IMO number), maintained by the ITU Radiocommunication Bureau based on notifications from national administrations ("Administrations") — this is the one genuinely primary, non-aggregator, international-institutional source for MMSI assignment identified in this entire research pass. — [ITU MARS page](https://www.itu.int/en/ITU-R/terrestrial/mars), [US Coast Guard NAVCEN](https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/node/33030)
- Attempting to query MARS directly at `https://www.itu.int/mmsapp/ShipStation/list?shipName=XUE+LONG` redirected (301) to `https://mmsapp.itu.int/mmsapp/ShipStation/list?shipName=XUE+LONG`, which rendered as a blank search form stating "No query criteria provided" / "Please enter at least one search criteria" — i.e., the query-string parameter name used was not the one the form actually expects (the search is likely a JS-driven POST), so no record was retrieved. — (tested directly, this research session)
- 雪龙号 (Xue Long, IMO 8877899, call sign BNSK): MMSI 412863000 is used by VesselFinder, VesselTracker, and a wiki mirror, which consistently describe it as an icebreaker/research vessel built 1993 in Kherson, Ukraine, home port Shanghai. MMSI 412018330 is used by MagicPort and Maritime Optima for the same IMO 8877899. The two aggregator "camps" disagree on both MMSI and on vessel-type classification (icebreaker vs. miscellaneous/special cargo), suggesting at least one of the two MMSIs may be stale/reassigned data rather than currently valid. — [VesselFinder](https://www.vesselfinder.com/vessels/details/8877899), [MagicPort (412018330)](https://magicport.ai/vessels/special-craft/xue-long-mmsi-412018330)
- 大洋一号 (Da Yang Yi Hao, IMO 8226961, call sign BQBC5 per the 412017140 record / BNTM per the 412923000 record): the MMSI 412017140 record shows a very recent AIS position fix ("less than 1h ago" at time of that page's snapshot), while the MMSI 412923000 record (VesselFinder) shows a position "335 days ago." This recency gap is suggestive — but not proof — that 412017140 is the currently active MMSI and 412923000 is a retired/reassigned one. — [Maritime Optima](https://maritimeoptima.com/public/vessels/pages/imo:8226961/mmsi:412017140/DA_YANG_YI_HAO.html), [VesselFinder (412923000)](https://www.vesselfinder.com/en/vessels/DA-YANG-YI-HAO-IMO-8226961-MMSI-412923000)
- 实验3号 (Shi Yan 3, IMO 8427046, call sign BQAC2): only MMSI 413621760 was found in this pass, consistently across MagicPort pages, attributed to owner/manager "CHINA GOVT INST OCEANOGRAPHY." No source mentioning MMSI 412988000 for this vessel turned up in this search. — [MagicPort](https://magicport.ai/vessels/unspecified/shi-yan-3-mmsi-413621760)

### Inferences
- The recency of the last-reported AIS fix (as surfaced by aggregator "last seen" timestamps) is a reasonable *heuristic* for which MMSI is currently live for 大洋一号 (favoring 412017140), but this is an inference from aggregator metadata, not an institutional confirmation, and should be labeled as such in any downstream report.
- For 雪龙号, no comparable recency signal was retrieved in this pass to break the tie between 412863000 and 412018330; both values appear across multiple aggregator families without one being clearly stale.
- Given that MMSI blocks starting with 412 are assigned to China (MID 412), both competing values for each vessel are at least structurally valid Chinese MMSIs, meaning the conflict is plausibly a genuine vessel-reflagging/re-registration history (old MMSI retired, new one issued) rather than one side being simply wrong/fabricated — this is consistent with how MMSIs are periodically reassigned, but was not confirmed by any administrative source (e.g., China MSA) in this pass.

### Gaps
- **实验3号's MMSI 412988000 could not be found or characterized at all** — it's unclear whether this value was itself a misread, a decommissioned prior assignment, or belongs to a different, similarly-named vessel. A follow-up researcher should try searching the bare number "412988000" directly (not paired with the ship name) to see what it resolves to.
- ITU MARS was *identified* as the single most authoritative tie-breaking source for all three conflicts but was **not successfully queried** in this pass — the form likely requires a session-based POST or JS form submission that a simple GET-with-query-string WebFetch cannot replicate. A follow-up with a tool capable of interacting with web forms (e.g., a browser-automation tool) should retry `https://mmsapp.itu.int/mmsapp/ShipStation/list` with the correct form field names (try submitting via the visible form fields rather than guessed URL parameters) for 雪龙 (Xue Long), 实验3号 (Shi Yan 3), and 大洋一号 (Da Yang Yi Hao) by ship name, call sign, or IMO number.
- No Chinese government source (China MSA / 中国海事局 ship registry, or 工业和信息化部 which administers MID 412 allocations domestically) was checked in this pass for an authoritative domestic MMSI registry equivalent to ITU MARS — this would be a natural next source to try, given 412 is China's MID block and domestic allocation records would sit with China's telecom/maritime regulator rather than with ITU alone.
